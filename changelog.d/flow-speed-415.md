- Each heating circuit's flow dashes follow its own flow rate (`hc_volume`, `hc2_volume` …) instead of the heat pump's `volume`; risers and the shared trunk follow the sum of the circuits they feed. Behind a parallel buffer a circuit without a flow rate moves at a neutral speed, without a buffer it keeps the heat pump's speed, as it is the same water loop; `flow_speed` still scales everything (#415).
- New entity `primary_pump_state` (on/off): the primary pump's own state, for heat pumps that report it. It decides whether the primary pump turns; a flow rate above zero no longer starts it on its own, as a circuit pump can push water through the heat pump's loop while its pump stands. The flow rate stays visible then. Without the entity, or while it is unavailable, the flow rate decides as before (#416).
